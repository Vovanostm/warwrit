# G10 pure combat/company composition

This packet joins existing economy, physical, learning, social and combat owners
in one deterministic candidate. It does not provide durable execution, world
arrival, physical two-company joining, or the playable M1 loop.

## Scope and retained boundaries

`prepareBeginCombatAggregate` binds the actual participants and initial physical
state. Receipt consumption validates the full journal and causal tick/evidence,
applies real wounds, death and practice with earned learning interruptions, and
advances one cursor only after every mandatory owner succeeds. Finalization binds
the terminal journal/state and all accepted evidence to its retry identity.

Terminal handling restores present participants at the same site, retains sourced
missing/captive custody, verifies deaths, resolves a source-bound leadership
crisis and executes one chosen lawful departure per eligible membership. Local
company-owned property follows existing return/overflow rules. There is no
automatic loot, reward, resurrection, teleport, or generated successor.

Capture requires a living bound participant, a matching capture outcome and every
referenced seizure from the same terminal source/subject/tick. It uses the existing
Capture and learning composition. A captured leader requires the actual
`LEADER_UNAVAILABLE` crisis and an eligible acting successor. Invalid evidence
rejects the whole candidate; identical retry retains the completed root and changed
capture/learning evidence conflicts.

# B5 — war crisis

The first world crisis is war between duchies with settlement capture.
Armies act in the shared world. Players lead mercenary companies.
Some army battles are resolved by the administrator, some by accepted simple rules.

The current direction belongs to [vision](../../../wiki/vision.md#кризисы)
and [world mechanics](../../../wiki/mechanics/armies.md).
A selected crisis slice defines real events, authorization, observations,
saved consequences and recovery in its own work package.

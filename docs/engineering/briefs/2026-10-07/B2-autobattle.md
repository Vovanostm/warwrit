# B2 — autobattle

Autobattle uses the same rules and legal commands as manual combat.
The owner selected retreat when appropriate and real injury/death consequences.
AI may take the company to a settlement and resolve encounters on that route.

[AI rules](../../../wiki/mechanics/opponents.md) and
[M1-CAMPAIGN-BATTLE](../../../work-packages/M1-CAMPAIGN-BATTLE.md)
own current tactical policy, versions and persistent outcomes.
The selected feature records the normal entry, control handoff, refusal,
retry and saved result. Readiness and delivery follow [AGENTS.md](../../../../AGENTS.md).

# B3 — repeatable contracts

Repeatable work is part of the long-term game direction and follows M1.
Current ordinary conditions belong to [M1-CONTRACT-TERMS](../../../work-packages/M1-CONTRACT-TERMS.md).
The current twenty-quest matrix belongs to [M1-QUESTS](../../../work-packages/M1-QUESTS.md).

A repeatable-contract slice must identify a real episode, issuer, objective,
funding source, unique claim and subsequent renewal conditions. Its concrete
values and implementation status belong to the selected work package.

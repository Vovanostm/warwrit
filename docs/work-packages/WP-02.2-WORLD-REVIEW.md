## Corrected failures

**LW-01 — real care, not a label.** A provider with `critical-bleed` was AVAILABLE
in the presence dimension and could receive a recovering field member. The result
freed the field slot despite the provider's existing `basicWork` prohibition.
The transition now requires actual availability and basic-work capability from the
same condition catalogue used for leadership and garrison eligibility. This is a
necessary prerequisite, not proof of paid treatment, skill, supplies or healing.
Those remain the existing care/settlement responsibilities of02.3–02.4.

**LW-02 — a confirmed world value cannot change through a borrowed reference.**
Opening locations, arrival destinations and recruitment item lists were retained
by reference from adapter evidence. Reusing that evidence could relocate a prepared
company or alter its transfer requirements without a new transition. The component
now owns copies of those retained values. This is an in-process aliasing defect,
not a demonstrated remote exploit. Unchanged canonical state can still use
structural sharing; cloning/freezing the whole persistent graph is unnecessary.

Both failures were reproduced through `prepareCompanyLifecycle` before correction:
15 passed /2 failed scenarios, with five failed assertions. After correction, all
29 focused specifications passed (17 lifecycle +12 unchanged foundation), along
with core/protocol builds, strict testkit typecheck, lint and architecture checks.
Final clean CI results belong in the delivery receipt for the exact published SHA;
these focused results do not substitute for that gate.

## Imagination with causality — illustrative scenes, not new canon

**The occupied sickroom.** A wounded companion reaches a settlement, but the named
recipient is also critically incapacitated. Relabelling the first as RECOVERY does
not free a slot. The company needs a genuinely able recipient and the applicable
care arrangement. This preserves the dilemma of protecting someone rather than
turning a menu label into a rescue.

**The delayed report.** The company knows only where its scout was last seen.
A later verified lifecycle disclosure can update that knowledge; decorative text
cannot reveal the scout's current prison, killer or thoughts. `COMPANY_OBSERVATION`
is an explicitly authorized lifecycle disclosure, not the producer for an ordinary
rumour. Rumour confidence/partial knowledge remain the world/narrative work packages.

**The disputed banner.** A real household successor takes command where they are.
The bypassed veteran reacts only on actual notification, once. A departure intent
is not an instant disappearance; wounds, encounter state, earned obligations and
item custody still govern the eventual departure. No new rival, feud or settlement
revolution is generated to make the scene more dramatic.

These are authored demonstrations of existing rules, not implemented quests,
new NPC identities, new decision options, art assets or passed playtests. The
working place names and mill chain in the lore remain provisional authored content.

## Minimal narrative handoff

Future presentation must distinguish the canonical occurrence, what this observer
actually learned, and authored wording. Reuse existing event/observation references;
do not create a parallel store of story truth. A missing witness, fact or location
must remain unknown, not be filled by an LLM. A company-origin label is not evidence
that every hire personally lived the leader's biography.

Before the first playable, exercise one existing route-to-contract-to-proof chain
and the care/succession cases on the available MacBook/Chrome. Measure whether
players understand the choices and consequences. Headless correctness alone does
not establish pacing, readability, emotional attachment, fun or server capacity.
No renderer, runtime narrator or new interview is needed to accept these fixes.

## Compatibility and verification boundaries

No payload, state shape, catalogue value, balance coefficient, combat V1 rule,
dependency, lockfile, migration, HTTP endpoint or production CI workflow changes.
Capability checking enforces the existing real-care prerequisite. Copying values
changes reference ownership, not serialized valid data. No version bump or migration
is needed for this unreleased correction; old invalid care transitions must not be
manufactured or accepted as a migration strategy.

`PREPARED` remains component calculation, not aggregate acceptance or publication.
Original specification ZIP-concordance remains NOT_RUN. This review does not claim
full WP-02, medical recovery, world travel, narrative production or multiplayer load
acceptance. Merge, deployment and paid resources are outside this task.

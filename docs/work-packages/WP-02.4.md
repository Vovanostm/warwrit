## B — Items, containers, equipment and local transfers

An item has a stable instance ID, definition reference, explicit owner,
physical custody container, exact positive quantity, current condition and
provenance. Use the existing `OwnerRef` and `LocationRef`. A weapon/book unique
instance has quantity one. Stack merging requires compatible definition, owner,
condition, content/batch and provenance; a split preserves a source-linked child
ID and exact parent remainder. Moving/retrying never remints the item. Terminal
consumption/destruction retains a source-linked audit/tombstone, not erased history.

Containers have real location/custodian/carrier, capacity, access policy and
kind. Carried/equipped items count once toward actual weight. No recursive bag
capacity bonus. Check all access, ownership, quantity, weight, slot/body and
encounter predicates **before** any change. An ordinary requested transfer that
does not fit rejects unchanged; don't auto-lower quantity or auto-sell overflow.
Personal property is not confiscated for wage debt. Wearing an item does not
transfer ownership. An explicit gift/sale/seizure needs its own real authorization.

`TransferItem`, `EquipItem`, `RepairItem`, `ClaimLoot` and
`ApplyContainerLifecycle` consume their existing commands. HEAD/BODY/MAIN_HAND/
OFF_HAND/BELT constraints come from the current body/item definitions. Two-handed
items occupy the correct hands; equipped gear must be actually accessible and
compatible. No equipment change during an active encounter. Re-equipping never
repairs armor or refills health/stamina. Repair requires actual local material or
an authorized paid provider and changes only existing current condition up to
its defined maximum. No item plus cash-equivalent double charge for one expense.

Victory is not automatic loot authority. Validate actual outcome, item identity,
current custody, local access, explicit claim authorization and destination
capacity together. Missing is not dead and does not generate a corpse/bundle.
Containers have no invented TTL: only a verified due `ApplyContainerLifecycle`
fact can transfer the same contents or destroy them with a retained cause.

## C — Real care, consumption and eligible recovery

Implement `ApplyCondition` and `ApplyCare`, with actual condition instances:
source/onset, definition, treatment prerequisite fulfilled by which effect,
eligible recovery accumulated/carry, resolved state and persistent history/scars.
Intersect restrictions through the same capability evaluator used by lifecycle
and maintenance. Availability, position, assignment, injury and ability are not
interchangeable. After changes, reconcile affected coverage using shared rules:
`basicWork` sustains FIELD_CAMP, `localDuty` admits a new F1 cohort; admitted
recovering groups do not suddenly incur ordinary charges.

Use corrected provisional profile 0.2 from S3/current definitions: minor recovery
50 ticks, severe stable **after real required care** 250, full-health rest basis
100, stamina rest basis 10; critical causal window 250 where that condition source
applies it. These are initial game parameters, not measured balance or new medicine
advice. Define any missing coefficients once in the versioned catalogue.

Rest-recoverable conditions need actual eligible food/rest/time. Treatment-required
stable conditions do not improve before actual care and do not randomly kill.
A critical condition must have its explicit causal source and deadline; real
on-time stabilization removes that critical cause and fulfills the already-given
care without a second charge. It does not instantly restore full health. Permanent
injuries and scars survive ordinary rest and administrative edits; exceptional
care needs existing permission/provider/resources. No resurrection, free hospital
or automatic extra severe disease in stable F1 service.

Consume `FOOD_CONSUMPTION` person-tick demand from economy exactly once, retaining
fractional carry across calls/reload. Real current F1/field support is not stocked
rations. Material and paid-provider channels are distinguishable fulfillment, not
two deductions. Resource/budget insufficiency must not claim performed care or
silently advance unsupported recovery. Uncovered demand does not invent starvation
death. Keep accumulated eligible progress, not per-call rounding.

Current HP/stamina/armor and maximum values are distinct. Give current pools a
single persistent owner and explicit source-backed initialization; never default
missing current health to full. Derive maxima in one place from actual supported
attributes/definitions; do not implement a second combat engine. The combat V2
projection owns its later mapping. Increase/clamp of a maximum is not free healing.

## D — Physical outcomes and knowledge

Consume verified `Capture`, `ReleaseCaptive`, `TransferCaptive`, `ResolveMissing`
and `RecordDeath` facts. Apply actual subject, location, custody and item effects
without inventing a captor, release route, arrival, casualty or proof. Captivity/
loss of contact suspend incompatible physical duties but do not erase membership,
old debt or kinship. Release is not instantaneous company return. Reuse real
`ReturnToService`/arrival/party-capacity rules; no remote join or inherited free gear.

Missing resolutions require their actual not-before boundary and outcome receipt;
unknown is not a null/default death. Death changes physical availability/custody,
closes actual earning at its real tick through the existing finance consumer and
keeps earned estate obligations. Personal/company property follows actual custody
and its existing ownership rule; do not convert every item automatically to
company loot. Leadership eligibility must use the resulting complete real graph,
not an emergency heir or hidden public candidate list. Do not reimplement RC-P1.

Order real causal boundaries, including condition/capability changes, not just
current arrays. Never infer historic capacity from today's condition list when
processing a delayed outcome. Late knowledge may disclose an earlier factual
outcome; a late command cannot change that earlier history. Same-source fan-out
must reach every distinct affected subject/item once, independent of rule version.

`Observe` discloses only what its scoped authorized evidence covers. Prior to
lawful disclosure, hidden death/capture/item disposition cannot release visible
money, reveal loot, alter colleagues' payouts, public slots or error/revision
behavior. Do not expose private receipt/requirement payloads as DTOs. Run paired
**sequences**, then disclose and reconcile once. Financial observations must
retain unavailable old-pool reserves; lack of remote cash access must not block a
valid local report or otherwise permissible departure.

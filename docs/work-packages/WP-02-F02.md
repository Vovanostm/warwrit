# WP-02 / F02 — bounded presentation service

Status: implementation contract for F02 only.

Authority: owner-approved S-02 identity rule + `WP02-SMALL-STEPS-v1` + canonical Commands/Lifecycle/Physical/Economy records.

## F02-v1 contract

The only cosmetic mutation activated by F02-v1 is a **barber hair-style change**. This is the narrowest executable interpretation of the approved rule that appearance can change through bounded services such as hair/barber services.

- mutable field: `hairStyleId` only;
- stored presentation shape: `{ schemaVersion: 1, hairStyleId }`;
- service contract: `BARBER_HAIR` / `s02-barber-hair-1`;
- allowed hair-style values come from the trusted local service evidence;
- the trusted evidence also binds character, provider, location, expiry, funding pool, provider wallet, money-access evidence and exact quoted price;
- no fixed barber tariff is introduced by F02;
- service evidence is single-use at the causal boundary;
- changing presentation and moving the quoted cash are one aggregate preparation: rejection returns the original state unchanged;
- the target must be an active company member, available, known to the company and physically at the service location;
- provider and local money access must be real and co-located.

## Explicitly not writable

`birthName`, sex, species, birth culture, birthplace, origin, birth time, kinship, physical conditions and scar history are outside the presentation surface. F02 does not create a generic face/body editor and does not reinterpret physical scars as cosmetics.

## Compatibility

`LifecycleCharacter.presentation` is optional so existing V1 snapshots remain representable. A successful F02 service materializes presentation state. Persistence migrations remain H-slice work.

## Deferred

Hair colour, facial hair, tattoos, makeup, body/face morphs, surgical changes and any other cosmetic category require a later versioned contract. A gameplay-significant expansion requires an explicit product delta rather than adding arbitrary keys to `appearancePatch`.

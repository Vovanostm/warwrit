- Bottom-left corner: only the edge of the crown city outskirts, not dominant.

World rules: the river Mutnya is ASH-GREY, silty, never blue. Serious grounded
dark fantasy; war traces are local (the broken span, the burnt mill, graves);
inhabited places look lived-in and worn, not ruined.

Style: ink linework and hatching over flat muted wash; palette slate, soot, umber,
muted ochre, faded olive, restrained rust accents. Weathered stone and timber,
irregular contours, roots and entrances grounded. Readable paths and crossings.

Avoid: text, letters, labels, legends, compass, frame; castles, tall white
towers, pristine pale masonry, stone-arch Karaulny bridge, blue water, vivid
green lawns, glossy surfaces, cute rounded shapes, fantasy creatures,
a working picturesque mill.

Format: landscape 3:2, the largest resolution the tool provides.
